# Mouthful privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Mouthful helps you practise what you'll say out loud. This page explains what happens to your voice, your words and your data. The short version: your practice stays on your phone.

## Your voice

- **Mouthful listens only while you practise**: when a reading, word drill or Free talk session is running.
- **Speech to text.** Your phone's speech recognition turns what you say into text so Mouthful can follow along.
  - When your phone can do this itself for the English you picked, your voice is not sent anywhere.
  - When it can't, Mouthful asks you first. If you agree, your audio goes to your phone's speech service while you practise: Apple on iPhone, or your phone's speech service (often Google) on Android. Their own privacy policies apply. You can withdraw this agreement at any time in **You → Privacy**.
- **Recordings** are kept only if you switch them on (they start off). They are saved only on your phone, in Mouthful's own folder. They are not uploaded by Mouthful. Like other app files, they may be included in your phone's own iCloud or Google backup if you use one.
- **Tester mode** (supervised beta only, off for everyone else) records every reading session and keeps what the phone heard, on the phone, so a tester and the developer can review it together.

## What stays on your phone

Your sessions, scores, scripts, word lists, settings and recordings are stored on your phone. Mouthful has no account and no server that holds them.

## Usage counts (analytics)

Mouthful can send anonymous usage counts to PostHog (USA) to see which parts of the app help, for example "session finished", a score, a duration or a word count. Each phone gets a random ID that isn't linked to your name, email or Apple ID.

- We **never** send your words, scripts, transcripts or voice. The app removes any text before sending.
- We **never** send the English variety you picked, since it could hint at where you're from.
- PostHog can see the internet address a request comes from. We don't use it to identify you.
- You can switch usage counts off in **You → Privacy**, and they stay off if you reset the app.

## Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple and RevenueCat. Mouthful never sees your payment details. RevenueCat receives a random app user ID and your purchase status so your Pro access works. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

## Deleting your data

- **You → Delete recordings** deletes every recording file on the phone.
- **You → Reset all data** deletes your sessions, scripts, words, settings and recordings.
- Deleting the app deletes everything Mouthful stored on the phone.
- Usage counts already sent can't be tied back to you, but you can ask us to delete them for your phone's ID: a.twist100@gmail.com.

## Children

Mouthful is not made for children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect anything from them.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above changes, and the app will tell you about anything important.
