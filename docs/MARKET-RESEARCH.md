# Review mining: what users love, ask for and hate (marketing-lead, 2026-10-29)

**Data:** 2,863 App Store reviews from the RSS feeds (US, CA, GB, IN; pages 1–10), feed format
`https://itunes.apple.com/{cc}/rss/customerreviews/page=N/id=<id>/sortby=mostrecent/json`. Apps: Wellspoken
(6752822613) 119, PromptSmart (894811756) 579, Speeko (1071468459) 29, Orai (1203178170) 198, ELSA (1083804886) 547,
Stimuler (1627501632) 229, Vocal Image (1535324205) 491, BoldVoice (1567841142; 53,954 ratings) 165, PatterAI
(6745208857) 154, MemoCoach (1220188894) 327, Interview Pilot (6743263009) 25. Perora and Sayglide: 0 reviews.

**Caveats:** themes coded by keyword search, so counts are approximate. Written reviews skew far more negative
than star averages. Reddit was blocked for fetch and search (no Reddit citations; Quinton could pull them by hand).

## Loved (4–5 star)

- **Memorising step by step** (MemoCoach, 143): "I have 3 days to learn 4 pages of lines".
- **Progress you can see** (BoldVoice 40, Orai 27, Wellspoken 23).
- **Short daily practice** (Wellspoken): "not overwhelming".
- **Instant playback plus targeted drills** (BoldVoice).
- **Filler and pace feedback** (PromptSmart pace 19, Orai pace 7); "Picks up filler words!" (Speeko).
- **Voice follow** (PromptSmart 19): "don't have to pick a speed and try to keep up".
- **A founder who answers** (Stimuler).

## Asked for (99 matches)

Saved answers to look back at; spaced re-tests with a success graph; a different passage each time; more job
types and longer interview answers; highlight where I left off, loop the script, bigger text; photograph my
script; a "self-consciousness" category; more guidance ("I have to spend effort trying to remember to do this app").

## Steal list (principle 1 always: no grades, no "pronounced correctly")

1. **Fade, 2 levels** (MemoCoach, Perora): the strongest love signal. M.
2. **One tap to hear your last run** (BoldVoice; missing in Vocal Image, ELSA). S–M. Held in memory, discarded
   unless the reader says yes (principle 3); release-privacy signs off.
3. **Target length with green/yellow/red** ("your answer: 2 min", Toastmasters). S.
4. **Saved runs you can replay** (Stimuler request). S.
5. **A fresh passage or prompt each day** (Speeko request). S, via One Minute.
6. **Write to the founder in the app** (Stimuler). S.
7. **Success graph and re-tests spaced toward the date** (MemoCoach). M; close to PlanCard.
8. **Scan Text tip** (iOS camera into the script box) (MemoCoach request). S.
9. **Mock questions for your own job** (Wellspoken). L; needs opt-in AI (dilemma 10).

Don't steal: BoldVoice's sound tutorials, ELSA's scores (pronunciation grading).

## Beat list

Billing (no-auto-renew Moment Pass, "Cancel in Settings" on the paywall, a trial reminder that fires); review
prompt (only after the second finished run or after the date); accents ("heard clearly" + overrule; no claim before
the beta); losing your place (resync, proven on phones); onboarding (moment and date, then reading; no
questionnaire); streaks (keep the heat strip, never a streak that resets).

## White space

- **"How did Thursday go?"** after the date: nobody follows up. S.
- **A plan counted down to a date.** "Plan" alone isn't ours (Vocal Image: "daily personalized plan… when it
  counts"), but "to your date" still is.
- **Calm framing for nervous speakers.**

## Hard truth

Only **14 of 2,863** reviews mention interviews; presentations (49) and lines (38) lead. Interview demand isn't
proven in the stores. Recruit beta testers with presentations too, and don't lock the headline to "interview"
until the beta shows which moment people pick.
