# Marketing, product and tools research (2026-11-01)

> From the research agent (sections saved as they arrived). Ideas, not decisions.

## 1. Slogans
**Top 3**
- **A. "Sound as sharp as you think."** Names the gap every user feels (your head is faster than your mouth); works for fluent and ESL speakers; 27 chars, fits the App Store subtitle.
- **B. "One minute a day. Clearer every week."** Sells the loop and the proof; the TikTok "day 7 of…" format. Paywall and ads.
- **C. "We count your ums. You fix them."** Honesty with personality; social only ("ums" is informal for ESL UI).
- Keep "Find the words. Say them clearly." as the secondary line (word packs, Clear English).
- Not yet checked for App Store or trademark collisions: search A and B before locking.

## 2. Positioning vs Wellspoken and Vozie
Evidence is thin: few documented Wellspoken complaints (Trustpilot mostly positive, https://www.trustpilot.com/review/wellspoken.me); the App Store critique is "needs more guidance, hard to keep up"; weekly $4.99, family annual $129.99 (https://apps.apple.com/app/id6752822613). No Vozie reviews found. Category complaints (unverified for these two, https://unstar.app/blog/is-speak-elsa-talkpal-loora-learna-ai-english-apps-legit-2026): sneaky weekly subscriptions, paywalled lessons, shallow repetition. Check the reviews yourself before using any of this in copy.

**One sentence:** Wellspoken coaches you through exercises; SaidClearly gives you one honest minute a day, scored on numbers you can check, and a map that shows you moving forward.

1. **"Real numbers, not a pep talk."** Fillers, repeats, start time, pace, Flow, next to yesterday. Show a result screen in every ad.
2. **"One minute. That is the whole habit."** A topic handed to you and a map that says where you are: answers "I don't know what to practise today".
3. **"Priced like a habit, not a trap."** One clear annual price, no weekly billing, a free daily minute. Don't claim cheapest (Vozie $25/yr); claim clearest and fairest.
- Bonus: **"We never grade your accent."** A trust point for Clear English.

## 3. Free or cheap tools (only ASO was searched; check every price on the vendor's page)
- **ASO:** store autocomplete + App Store Connect + Google Trends ($0) is enough for the first title and keywords; Appfigures free plan or ASOMobile for a few tracked keywords. Skip AppTweak / Sensor Tower. (Leads: https://appdrift.co/blog/best-free-aso-tools-2026 · https://trysonar.app/blog/best-free-aso-tools)
- **Landing + waitlist:** Carrd (free; ~$19/yr Pro for a domain and forms) or the existing GitHub Pages site + a free Tally form (docs/marketing/WAITLIST-PAGE.md).
- **Video:** CapCut free (captions, cuts); schedule with TikTok's own scheduler, Buffer free (3 channels) or Metricool free. Label AI faces; never fake user testimonials.
- **Analytics / crashes / payments:** PostHog and Sentry already in; RevenueCat free up to $2.5k monthly revenue. Add nothing else.
- **Feedback:** Calendly/Meet + a Telegram or Discord tester group; a Tally "what almost made you quit?" form; TestFlight (needs the $99 Apple account).
- **Design:** Figma free for store screenshots; Phosphor or Lucide icons. (Correction from our own research: Rive's free plan can't export; see docs/research/RIVE.md.)
- **Email:** Loops or MailerLite free tier; pick one.

## 4. Product ideas (all inside the honesty rules and the one-minute loop)
| Idea | Why users care | Effort | Risk / guard |
|---|---|---|---|
| Before/after card: Day 1 vs Day 30 on the same topic | Proof you improved; the most shareable thing | M | Audio needs consent: default to stats only |
| Same-topic friend challenge by link | Social pull without accent judgement | M–L (backend) | Compare fillers/Flow only, private link |
| "Beat yesterday": one personal-best number to beat | Clear daily goal | S | Streak anxiety: freeze |
| Filler replay: tap an "um", hear 3 s around it | Makes the score believable | M | Recogniser errors: "detected", dismissible |
| One Word Swap: 3 vague words you used ("good", "thing") with sharper options | The "find the words" promise | M | Curated list, never a free-form model |
| Topic of the day shared by everyone (Wordle-style) + share card | Daily ritual and conversation | S–M | Topics safe in every region |
| Say-it-again: redo the same minute, see the change | Fastest visible win | S | Optional |
| Weekly articulation report | Retention; a reason to keep paying | S–M | Only with 3+ talks |

## 5. Growth loops ($0)
1. **Share card:** after each talk a clean Stories/TikTok card ("Day 12: fillers 14 → 6, Flow 78") with the topic and app name. First step: card + share sheet on results; measure shares per active user in PostHog.
2. **Daily topic thread:** post the day's topic in ESL/speaking communities and a Telegram group; people reply with cards. First step: read each subreddit's rules, post 5 helpful prompts with no link; start a Telegram group with 10 waitlist testers.
3. **Build in public + small creators:** film your own 30-day challenge with real result screens; offer free lifetime codes to 10 small ESL/speaking creators (1k–20k). First step: record Day 1 today and send 10 DMs. Never pay for fake testimonials; disclose AI presenters.
- Later: "invite a friend, both get a week of word packs" via RevenueCat promo codes, once loop 1 works.

## Do this first (ranked)
1. Lock the hero line: check "Sound as sharp as you think" and "One minute a day. Clearer every week." for App Store collisions; use as subtitle and TikTok hook.
2. Build the result share card (stats before/after, no audio) and track shares in PostHog: the only loop that grows by itself.
3. Record Day 1 of your own 30-day challenge and post it ($0; the content engine).
4. Waitlist page + form + a 10-person Telegram tester group (early users and real quotes).
5. Cheap features that make the loop visible: one shared topic of the day + Say-it-again; then the weekly report.
