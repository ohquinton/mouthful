## Description
Sound sharp. Say what you mean.
SaidClearly gives you one topic a day. Talk about it for a minute, get a Flow rating, and try to beat it tomorrow.
Find precise words, cut the ums, and keep a steady pace. Small daily reps, tracked over weeks.

HOW IT WORKS
• Today's topic: coffee, luck, the bus. One minute, out loud. The first 8 seconds of thinking don't count against you.
• Your Flow rating: whether you kept talking, with no long pauses. It isn't a grade of your accent or your English.
• The details: ums and uhs the phone heard (phones miss some), the word you repeated most, how fast you started, and your pace in a suggested range. Likely fillers like "like" are pointed out, and you can tap any you meant.
• Different words this week: how varied your words were across your recent talks. Compare it only with yourself.

TWO LINES, ONE APP
• Sharp Talk: precise words, fewer fillers and repeats, a steady pace.
• Clear English: for people who speak English as another language and want to be understood the first time. Everyday words, "say this, not that".
Pick one when you start. Switch any time. Each day is a new stop on your line: a topic talk, a word pack or a read-aloud rehearsal where your words ink in as you say them.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account.
• It listens only while you talk. When your phone can turn speech into text by itself, your voice stays on the phone. When it can't, SaidClearly asks before using your phone's speech service.
• Recordings are off unless you turn them on, and stay on your phone.

WHAT IT DOESN'T DO
SaidClearly doesn't grade pronunciation or accents, and doesn't check grammar. It shows what the phone heard and counts what it can count.
