## Not different

- Words following your voice (PromptSmart since 2014; Sayglide free, with resync).
- Green as you say, red for missed, focus words, wpm, fillers, on-device, no account (Perora, free).
- On-device privacy isn't unique.
- "Glide" reads as a copy of Sayglide.

## Different, and true today

1. **A plan to a dated moment** (learn the words → in context → rehearse → cold read). Nobody else plans to a date.
2. **Honesty about recognition** ("heard clearly", overrule a red word) vs "pronunciation" grades.
3. **Delivery feedback with per-context suggested ranges**, steadiness, tripped words plus Word Packs to practise them.
4. **Pricing that doesn't trick you**, if kept: your own script free before the paywall; a no-auto-renew Moment Pass.

## Positioning

"Rehearse what you'll say at work, out loud, and be ready by the day it counts."

Proof points: your words ink in as you say them, and you can overrule any red word; a plan to your date; it
listens on your phone when your phone can, and asks before any audio leaves. No result claims until beta data.
