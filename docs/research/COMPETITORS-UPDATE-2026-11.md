## What changed for us
**Daily one-minute impromptu + precise words is no longer empty ground.** Wellspoken (big) and Vozie (tiny, cheap) both do it. Our edge now: honest numbers (no fake score), a generous free tier, on-device recognition, the two-door map. We can't be "cheapest": Vozie is $24.99/yr.
